PROJECT 01 · POWER BI

Sales & Profitability Analytics

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales and profitability performance across customers, products, and markets. The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, sales quantity, profit margins, and revenue and profit contributions.

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Power BI. It provides interactive visualizations to understand revenue trends, customer and product performance, and profitability across different markets.

Key Analysis

  • Revenue and sales quantity analysis
  • Revenue by customer and product
  • Revenue trends over time
  • Profit margin analysis by market
  • Profit margin analysis by customer
  • Revenue and profit contribution
  • Interactive year and month filtering
